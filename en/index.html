Frequently asked questions

I have never programmed. Can I do it?

Yes. Start with the from-scratch lessons: they need no experience and nothing to install.

Is it paid?

No, the whole site is free.

Do I need to learn Django and Flask at once?

No. It's better to pick one framework, build a simple project with it and only then compare it with the other one.

Can I build websites without a database?

Yes, but most real projects store users, products or content, so a database soon becomes necessary.

What matters more: theory or practice?

Practice. Theory helps you understand what is going on, but the skill only comes from writing code and working through your mistakes.
